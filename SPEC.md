#Spec v1: Reconcile Check

## What it does
Adds a structured status alongside Renée’s visit notes, showing each school’s relevant grant metric and an on-track/off-track status. This gives Stacy a quick view of where each school stands without replacing Renée’s detailed notes.

## Inputs
Renée’s visit information for each school
The relevant grant metric and current status for each school

## Outputs
For each school, display alongside Renée’s notes:
Key grant metric
On-track/off-track status
Renée’s original visit notes

## Done when
Stacy can review the status from her desk and immediately determine where each school stands, without needing to ask Renée in person or wait for her to return from a visit.
